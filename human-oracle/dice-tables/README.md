# Dice Tables

Detailed spin-direction interpretation tables for each element across all three dice colors.

Each die has 6 possible elements × 2 spin directions = 12 interpretive states. Combined across three dice, a single reading produces a coordinate in a space of 12 × 12 × 12 = 1,728 possible configurations (before card combinations).

## Quick Reference: Base Meanings (without spin)

| Element | Gold (Impulse) | Black (Distortion) | White (Liberation) |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **1-Fire** | Isolation, loneliness, desolation | Obsessiveness, compulsion, consumerism | Discriminating wisdom, compassion |
| **2-Air** | Vulnerability, anxiety, panic | Envy, suspicion, jealousy, paranoia | Spontaneous accomplishment, fluid action |
| **3-Earth** | Insecurity, fragility, emptiness | Arrogance, pride, fixity, miserliness | Equanimity, balance, harmony, generosity |
| **4-Water** | Fear of recognized threats | Anger, aggression, hatred | Clarity, mirror-wisdom, penetrating insight |
| **5-Vortex** | Chaos, instability, loss | Destruction, fragmentation, disorder | Renewal, transformation, liberation |
| **6-Star** | Restlessness, pursuit of growth | Hubris, megalomania, overconfidence | Enlightenment, growth, positive ambition |

## Spin Principle (applies to all dice)

- **Up ↑**: Energy directed outward — engaging, projecting, influencing environment, sharing
- **Down ↓**: Energy directed inward — reflecting, processing, self-confronting, cultivating

The specific flavor of "outward" and "inward" differs by dice color: Gold-up reaches toward the environment to stabilize or engage; Black-up projects shadow material outward creating friction; White-up radiates medicine to others.

## Source

Elemental framework adapted from: Khandro Dechen & Ngakpa Chögyam, *Spectrum of Ecstasy: Embracing the Five Wisdom Emotions of Vajrayana Buddhism*, Shambhala Publications, 2003.

Note: The original Vajrayana framework uses five elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space/Void). DELPHI maps these to a d6 by splitting Space into Vortex (destructive/transformative aspect) and Star (expansive/transcendent aspect), creating a six-element cycle.
