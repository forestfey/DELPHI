# Human Oracle — The Wheel

The Human divination method uses three colored dice mapped to elemental tantra psychology, with coin-toss spins adding directional flow to each result.

## The Three Dice

| Dice | Color | Role | Sanskrit Quality |
|------|-------|------|-----------------|
| **Gold** | Gold/Yellow | Initial Reaction to Space / Impulse | Rajas |
| **Black** | Iridescent/Dark | Distorted Energy | Tamas |
| **White** | White | Liberated Energy / Medicine | Sattva |

Each die is a standard d6. The number rolled maps to one of six elements.

## Element Mappings (d6)

| Roll | Element | Color |
|------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Fire | Red |
| 2 | Air | Green |
| 3 | Earth | Yellow |
| 4 | Water | Blue/White |
| 5 | Vortex/Spiral | Black/Purple |
| 6 | Sun/Star | Gold/Silver |

## Spin Directions

After each die roll, a coin toss determines the spin:

- **Up (↑)**: Energy directed outward, active, projecting, engaging with environment
- **Down (↓)**: Energy directed inward, reflective, processing, self-confronting

The spin transforms the meaning significantly. For example, Earth-3 in the Black (distortion) die:
- **Up**: Projecting internal rigidity outward — unyielding, domineering behavior
- **Down**: Turning inward to confront personal arrogance or attachment to rigid patterns

## Elemental Tantra Psychology

The interpretive framework draws from Vajrayana Buddhist psychology (particularly *Spectrum of Ecstasy* by Khandro Dechen and Ngakpa Chögyam), mapping each element across three states:

### Quick Reference

| Element | Gold (Impulse) | Black (Distortion) | White (Liberation) |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Earth** | Insecurity, fragility, emptiness | Arrogance, pride, fixity, miserliness | Equanimity, balance, harmony, generosity |
| **Water** | Fear of recognized threats | Anger, aggression, hatred | Clarity, mirror-wisdom, penetrating insight |
| **Fire** | Isolation, loneliness, desolation | Obsessiveness, compulsion, consumerism | Discriminating wisdom, compassion |
| **Air** | Vulnerability, anxiety, panic | Envy, suspicion, jealousy, paranoia | Spontaneous accomplishment, fluid action |
| **Vortex** | Chaos, instability, loss | Destruction, fragmentation, disorder | Renewal, transformation, liberation |
| **Star** | Restlessness, pursuit of growth | Hubris, megalomania, overconfidence | Enlightenment, growth, positive ambition |

For complete spin-direction interpretations per element per dice color, see [`dice-tables/`](dice-tables/).

## How to Roll

1. **Ask a question** — can be anything from mundane to philosophical
2. **Roll three d6** — one for each dice color (Gold, Black, White)
3. **Flip a coin** for each die — heads = up, tails = down (or your preferred mapping)
4. **Look up** the element (by number), the dice color (impulse/distortion/liberation), and the spin direction
5. **Interpret** using the tantra psychology tables and the specific spin-direction guidance

The dice can be used as a standalone "navigation check" or combined with the [Fey Oracle](../fey-oracle/) for a full DELPHI reading.

## Hardware

- Standard plastic d6 (physical roll; no digital RNG)
- 5-Eurocent copper coin (or any physical coin for spins)

The use of physical randomness generators is a deliberate methodological choice, not an aesthetic one.
