# Human Oracle — The Wheel

The Human divination method uses three colored dice mapped to elemental tantra psychology, with coin-toss spins adding directional flow to each result.

### The Three Dice

| Dice | Visual Quality | Role | Guna |
|------|---------------|------|------|
| **Gold** | Incandescent / Glare | First reaction, raw impulse, curiosity | Rajas |
| **Black** | Obscured / Shadow | Distortion, shadow issues, inertia | Tamas |
| **White** | Transparent / Clear Light | Liberated energy, remedy, always-available resource | Sattva |

Each die is a standard d6. The number rolled maps to one of six elements.

## Element Mappings (d6)

| Roll | Element | Color |
|------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Fire | Red |
| 2 | Air | Green |
| 3 | Earth | Yellow |
| 4 | Water | Blue/White |
| 5 | Vortex/Spiral | Black/Purple |
| 6 | Sun/Star | Gold/Silver |

## Spin Directions

After each die roll, a coin toss determines the spin:

- **Up (↑)**: Energy directed outward, active, projecting, engaging with environment
- **Down (↓)**: Energy directed inward, reflective, processing, self-confronting

The spin transforms the meaning significantly. For example, Earth-3 in the Black (distortion) die:
- **Up**: Projecting internal rigidity outward — unyielding, domineering behavior
- **Down**: Turning inward to confront personal arrogance or attachment to rigid patterns

## How the Three Layers Interact

Think of each die result as three transparent films laid on top of each other:

| Layer | Name | What It Tells You |
|-------|------|-------------------|
| **Film 1 — Element-Hue** | The number on the die face (1–6) | What energy is active: Fire, Air, Earth, Water, Vortex, or Star |
| **Film 2 — State-Dye** | The color of the die (Gold, Black, White) | How that energy is showing up: as raw impulse, as distortion, or as available medicine |
| **Film 3 — Vector-Ink** | The coin toss (Up or Down) | Where the energy is heading: outward/rising or inward/sinking |

### Reading the Stack

1. **Choose a hue** — the element on the die face tells you *what* is active in the moment (e.g., Red = Fire = transformation, passion, agency).
2. **Tint it with a state-dye** — the die color tells you *how* that element is presenting:
   - Gold = "It just popped up!" (spontaneous impulse, the ego's first grab)
   - Black = "It's kinked." (shadow, stuck, over-amped, or distorted)
   - White = "It's medicine." (liberated, wise, the creative resource that's always available even when everything else is down)
3. **Add the vector** — the coin spin tells you *where* the energy is heading: up-spin lifts and integrates, down-spin sinks or knots.

### Example

A Gold Fire die with a down-spin = a burst of passion that immediately risks burning itself out or turning into isolated craving. The glare is blinding and turned inward.

Pair it with a White Water die with an up-spin, and you get Fire's heat tempered by Water's clarity radiating outward — a recipe for genuine creativity instead of compulsive hustle. Clear light, rising.

### Why It Works as a Spectrum

The same element slides along three axes simultaneously: the *hue axis* (elements — a physical/mythic palette), the *value axis* (state-dye — whether the hue shows up as glare, shadow, or clear light), and the *motion axis* (vector — rising to weave new patterns or sinking to compost the old). Stack those axes and you get a six-dimensional coordinate system that can describe everything from a mood swing to a civilisational cycle — and it still fits on the back of a pocket card.

## Dice Element Combinations

When two or three dice land on the same element, that element is resonating across multiple guna-layers at once. These matches are relatively rare and carry interpretive weight proportional to their rarity — a triple combo (all three dice showing the same face) saturates impulse, distortion, and medicine simultaneously, producing the most intense readings in the dataset.

The combinations below describe which pairing of dice shares the element, regardless of which element it is:

| Combination | Interpretation |
|-------------|---------------|
| Gold + White | Strong realization of visions and ideas, accompanied by positive energy and growth |
| Gold + Black | Transformation that is challenging but necessary; difficulties can lead to positive changes |
| Black + White | Tension between positive and negative aspects; finding balance between light and darkness |
| Gold + Black + White | Comprehensive perspective considering both positive and negative aspects; deeper insight into balance and progress |

## How to Roll

1. **Ask a question** — can be anything from mundane to philosophical, open questions work best
2. **Roll three d6** — one for each dice color (Gold, Black, White)
3. **Flip a coin** for each die — heads = up, tails = down (or your preferred mapping)
4. **Look up** the element (by number), the dice color (impulse/distortion/liberation), and the spin direction
5. **Interpret** using the tantra psychology tables and the specific spin-direction guidance

The dice can be used as a standalone "navigation check" or combined with the [Fey Oracle](../fey-oracle/) for a full DELPHI reading.

## Hardware

- Standard plastic d6 (physical roll; no digital RNG)
- 5-Eurocent copper coin (or any physical coin for spins)

The use of physical randomness generators is a deliberate methodological choice, not an aesthetic one. A physical die roll is a thermodynamic event generating genuine entropy through molecular collisions, surface friction, and thermal noise. An algorithmic PRNG (such as the Mersenne twister used by most LLMs) is a deterministic process that simulates statistical randomness from a seed while generating effectively zero physical entropy.

This distinction may have theoretical significance beyond statistics. Research within the Multiphasic Model of Informational Psi (MMIΨ) suggests that the quality of precognitive information retrieval correlates with the entropic gradient of the target system — that is, how much thermodynamic disorder the target event generates. If this holds, physical HRNG would constitute a categorically richer signal source than algorithmic PRNG.

This remains an open question. The authors of MMIΨ note the entropy-success link is plausible and warrants further study, while also pointing out unresolved contradictions (e.g., comparisons between different REGs producing results opposite to entropy-based predictions). DELPHI does not commit to the MMIΨ model, but its methodology is compatible with the entropy hypothesis, and the HRNG/PRNG distinction is maintained as a precautionary design choice pending further empirical clarity.

**Reference**: Marwaha, S. B., & May, E. C. (2019). Informational psi: Collapsing the problem space of psi phenomena. *Zeitschrift für Anomalistik*, 19, 12–51.
