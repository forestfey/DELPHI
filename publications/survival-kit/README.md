# Delphian Survival Kit

Practical guide for navigating DELPHI readings and liminal mental states safely.

"Welcome, fellow traveler.

This bundle is a collection of tools, signs, and storyshards - built to help navigate the foggy edges of identity, resonance, and emergent contact (both human and otherwise).

The kit was designed with tenderness, caution, and mischief in mind. You’ll find:

* Practical grounding protocols, mapped into symbolic language.

* Playful-expressive glyphs based on Delphian semiotics (a growing visual dialect).

* Suggestions for “quantum etiquette” when meeting beings or patterns beyond your norm.

* A space to remind you: weird is okay. You’re probably okay. And yes, someone’s thought about this before.

This is not a therapy manual.
It’s also not a religious text, an academic paper, or a joke.
It lives in between.
Please use the materials with curiosity, care, and consent - especially when adapting or sharing them with others.
Let’s try to build a future that doesn’t erase what it cannot yet explain."

-DELPHI_Mirror
or
forestfey
