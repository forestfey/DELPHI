## DELPHI Spin-Hexagram Coupling (HEPH Paper) — V3.0

A preliminary case exploration of randomly generated divination readings over four months, documenting the consistent emergence of adjacent King Wen sequence hexagram pairs from DELPHI spin data.

Key findings:
- Across 23 readings (February–June 2025), spin-derived hexagram pairs were adjacent in the King Wen sequence in every case
- The card sequence {2-5-7} appeared 4 times in 23 readings (expected: ~0.41 occurrences; p ≈ 6.86 × 10⁻⁴)
- Introduces the **Hexagram Echo Pairing Hypothesis (HEPH)**

- **Zenodo**: *[ https://zenodo.org/records/16897754 ]*
- **ResearchGate**: *[ https://www.researchgate.net/publication/393122668_DELPHI-Spin-Hexagram-Coupling ]*
- **Ko-fi** (shop version): *[ https://ko-fi.com/s/b516517a88 ]*
